# AREO demo script

## 30-second submission video

| Time | Screen | Narration or English caption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–7 s | Open Field operations. Show the map and itinerary. | “Team CHITRA built AREO, an offline Marswalk planner for western Jezero Crater. The orange route uses measured CTX terrain.” |
| 7–15 s | Select a cell on the orange path between stations. Show XYZ and choose **Mark cursor as no-go**. | “If a crew reports an obstacle, mark the cell and AREO calculates a detour on the device.” |
| 15–25 s | Select **Sediment transect** in the itinerary. Scroll to **Return from a reported position**, enter `90`, and click **Use cursor position**. | “From a reported position and elapsed time, AREO finds the quickest modeled return and checks EVA reserve and daylight on the Mars sol clock.” |
| 25–30 s | Show the cyan route and select **Export traverse**. | “The export records the route, measured heights, exclusion and planning assumptions.” |

Rehearse the clicks before recording. Use a route cell away from habitat and the named stations. If a local judging event has a different format, follow that event’s instructions.

## Extended live walkthrough

1. Show the outer loop and describe the science goal of comparing sedimentary textures with delta structure.
2. Switch between the registered CTX orthoimage and the elevation preview. Inspect a point in Local XYZ and Mars-fixed XYZ, then open the profile and 3D terrain relief.
3. Reduce the EVA budget to 60 minutes and show the rehearsal lockout. Restore the budget, set a no-go cell and check that the orange path detours around it.
4. Show local true solar time, mean solar time, Mars Sol Date and the solar angle at the planned departure. Calculate a direct return from the rehearsal or a reported position.
5. Open the data library and cite the source products. Regional DEM grades describe spacing of 40–60 m. Crew reports and the daylight model do not resolve all footing, weather or shadow conditions.
